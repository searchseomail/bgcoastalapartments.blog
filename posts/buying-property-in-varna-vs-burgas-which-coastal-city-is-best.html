Buying Property in Varna vs Burgas: Which Coastal City is Best?

RetroRider
10.08.2026 • Read time: 4 min

Bulgaria’s Black Sea coastline has long captured the imagination of foreign investors, retirees, and digital nomads seeking an affordable Mediterranean-style lifestyle with lower overall living costs. Among the many coastal gems, two major metropolitan hubs stand out as the ultimate contenders for property buyers: Varna, often dubbed the "Sea Capital" of Bulgaria, and Burgas, the dynamic southern gateway known for its industrial progress, expansive parks, and rapid urban revitalization. Choosing between these two powerhouses is not just a matter of picking a beach view; it requires a deep dive into lifestyle preferences, long-term economic trajectories, infrastructure, and real estate market dynamics.

Whether you are dreaming of a sleek modern apartment overlooking the Sea Garden, a lucrative buy-to-let investment, or a permanent relocation spot, comparing Varna and Burgas will help clarify which of these thriving coastal cities aligns best with your property goals.

Varna: The Cultural and Educational Sea Capital

Varna is Bulgaria’s third-largest city and unquestionably the cultural, historical, and academic heartbeat of the northern Black Sea coast. Boasting a rich history that stretches back millennia—exemplified by the Varna Necropolis, home to the oldest processed gold in the world—the city seamlessly blends ancient heritage with a vibrant, youthful energy fueled by its numerous universities.

Lifestyle and Vibe in Varna

Life in Varna revolves around the Sea Garden (Morska Gradina), a magnificent, sprawling park that runs parallel to the coastline, offering shaded walkways, open-air theaters, an aquarium, and direct access to sandy beaches. The city feels authentically metropolitan, remaining active and lively even during the cold winter months. Unlike smaller seasonal resort towns where everything shuts down after September, Varna maintains a robust year-round economy. Cafes, theaters, restaurants, and shopping malls stay packed with locals, making it an ideal choice for expats who want a bustling urban environment without sacrificing proximity to the sea.

Varna’s Real Estate Market Overview

The property market in Varna is diverse, catering to a wide array of budgets and preferences. Buyers can find everything from Austro-Hungarian architectural masterpieces in the historic center to prefabricated socialist-era panel blocks in residential neighborhoods like Mladost, as well as ultra-modern residential developments in suburbs such as Trakata and Vinitsa.

Because Varna is a major university town and commercial hub, demand for rental properties remains consistently high throughout the entire year. Investors looking for steady, long-term rental yields rather than purely seasonal tourism income often find Varna to be a safer bet. However, prime real estate directly overlooking the Sea Garden or situated in the central grid commands a significant premium, frequently rivaling prices found in parts of Sofia.

Burgas: The Southern Hub of Modernity and Green Living

Situated on the southern Black Sea coast, Burgas has undergone a remarkable renaissance over the past decade. Once perceived primarily as an industrial and transport hub dominated by its port and oil refinery, municipal leadership has transformed Burgas into one of the cleanest, most progressive, and family-friendly cities in Bulgaria. It frequently wins national accolades for the "Best City to Live in Bulgaria."

Lifestyle and Vibe in Burgas

Burgas offers a slightly more relaxed, orderly, and pedestrian-centric pace compared to Varna. The city’s Sea Garden is impeccably maintained, sloping gently down to wide, safe beaches. Burgas is also uniquely framed by a stunning necklace of natural lakes—Atanasovsko, Vaya, and Mandra—making it a paradise for nature lovers, kite-surfers, and birdwatchers. The city's compact center is remarkably walkable, featuring vibrant pedestrian zones filled with cafes, boutiques, and outdoor art installations. While it also features a year-round population, its rhythm feels distinctly more grounded and community-oriented.

Burgas’s Real Estate Market Overview

The real estate market in Burgas has seen explosive growth, driven by massive infrastructural upgrades, new bicycle lanes, pedestrian zones, and improved public transport. Neighborhoods like Lazur and Zornitsa, which offer proximity to both the Sea Garden and the central amenities, are highly sought after by buyers. Further inland, newer residential complexes in Slaveykov and Izgrev offer modern builds with great connectivity.

While property prices in central Burgas have risen steadily, they occasionally offer slightly better value per square meter compared to prime locations in Varna. Furthermore, Burgas serves as the ideal springboard for exploring nearby upscale coastal communities, and buyers interested in adjacent luxury developments often research opportunities like Real Estate in Sveti Vlas: Luxury Communities & Off-Season Living before making a final regional commitment.

Financial Considerations and Hidden Expenses

When calculating your budget for purchasing coastal property in Bulgaria, looking strictly at the listing price is a critical mistake. Both Varna and Burgas share the same national legal and tax frameworks, meaning transaction costs, notary fees, local municipality taxes, and agency commissions apply equally.

Savvy international buyers always factor in preliminary legal due diligence, translation services, and post-purchase utility transfers. To avoid unpleasant surprises down the line, it is essential to review a comprehensive breakdown of Hidden Costs and Taxes When Buying Real Estate in Bulgaria: What to Budget For before signing any preliminary contracts.

Additionally, while Varna and Burgas represent major urban centers, many buyers looking for lower entry points are tempted by ultra-cheap holiday complexes further down the coast. However, before jumping on a heavily discounted holiday flat, consider the long-term liquidity issues outlined in analyses like Is Buying a Studio in Sunny Beach Still a Good Idea?, which highlights why investing in established cities like Varna or Burgas often yields better capital preservation.

Accessibility and Infrastructure

Both cities boast international airports, making them easily accessible from major European capitals, particularly during the summer charter season.

  • Varna Airport (VAR): Situated just a short drive from the city center, it serves as a crucial transit point for the northern coast, connecting to destinations across Europe, the Middle East, and domestic flights to Sofia.
  • Burgas Airport (BOJ): Located in Sarafovo (technically part of the Burgas municipality), it handles millions of passengers annually, offering exceptional convenience if you plan to explore the southern coastal resorts like Sozopol, Nessebar, or Primorsko.

In terms of road infrastructure, Varna is connected to Sofia via the Hemus Highway (though sections are still under long-term construction), while Burgas is directly linked to the capital via the modern Trakia Highway, making driving times to Sofia from Burgas notably faster and smoother.

Which City Should You Choose?

Your ultimate choice between Varna and Burgas depends entirely on your lifestyle priorities and investment strategy:

Choose Varna if:

  • You prefer a larger, deeply historic, and culturally dense metropolitan environment.
  • You want strong, reliable year-round rental demand driven by universities and multinational corporate offices.
  • You love classic architecture mixed with modern urban parks.

Choose Burgas if:

  • You value modern urban planning, exceptionally clean public spaces, and bicycle-friendly infrastructure.
  • You prefer a slightly more tranquil, family-oriented atmosphere with seamless access to nature reserves and southern beaches.
  • You are looking for strong property value growth tied to aggressive municipal modernization.

Both Varna and Burgas offer an incredible Mediterranean-style quality of life at a fraction of the cost found in Western Europe. By weighing your personal needs against the unique character of each city, you can secure a valuable piece of Bulgaria’s beautiful Black Sea coastline that will serve you well for decades to come.
