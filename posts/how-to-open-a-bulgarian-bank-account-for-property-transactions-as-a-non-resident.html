Bulgarian Banking

How to Open a Bulgarian Bank Account for Property Transactions as a Non-Resident

SolarFlareX
04.08.2026 • Read time: 6 min

Purchasing real estate in Bulgaria as a non-resident is an attractive prospect for many international buyers, thanks to affordable property prices, picturesque coastal and mountain regions, and a relatively straightforward legal framework for EU and non-EU citizens alike. However, before you can finalize any purchase—whether you are eyeing a cozy apartment on the Black Sea coast or a rural villa—you will need a local financial foothold. Opening a Bulgarian bank account is not just a matter of convenience; it is a critical practical requirement for transferring purchase funds, paying local property taxes, and managing ongoing utility bills.

Navigating the Bulgarian banking sector as a non-resident requires preparation, patience, and an understanding of strict anti-money laundering (AML) regulations. This comprehensive guide walks you through everything you need to know to successfully open a bank account in Bulgaria tailored for property transactions.

Why You Need a Local Bank Account for Bulgarian Real Estate

While theoretically, international wire transfers can be made directly from a foreign bank account to a seller's account, having a local Bulgarian bank account simplifies the entire property acquisition lifecycle:

  • Notary Requirements: Bulgarian notaries usually require transactions to be executed via bank transfer, and having a local account ensures smooth, traceable domestic clearing without heavy international intermediary fees.
  • Utility Management: Once you own the property, setting up direct debits for electricity, water, and maintenance fees is infinitely easier with a domestic account.
  • Future Resale: When you eventually decide to sell your property, having an active account in Bulgaria makes repatriating or reinvesting your funds much simpler.

Choosing the Right Bank in Bulgaria

Bulgaria features a stable, EU-regulated banking system supervised by the Bulgarian National Bank (BNB) and aligned with European Banking Authority standards. Major players include UniCredit Bulbank, DSK Bank, Postbank (Eurobank Bulgaria), and Fibank (First Investment Bank). When selecting a bank for your property transaction, consider the following criteria:

  • English-Speaking Staff: Look for branches in major cities (Sofia, Varna, Burgas) or coastal hubs that regularly deal with foreign nationals.
  • Non-Resident Policies: Some banks are more welcoming to non-residents than others. Smaller banks may impose stricter internal compliance checks, while tier-one banks have dedicated international client desks.
  • Fees for International Transfers: Compare incoming and outgoing wire fees, as well as monthly maintenance costs for non-resident accounts.

Step-by-Step Process to Open the Account

Opening a bank account as a non-resident typically involves the following milestones:

1. Gather Your Documentation

Bulgarian banks enforce rigorous Know Your Customer (KYC) protocols. Expect to provide:

  • A valid passport (with clear copies of the photo page and entry stamps if applying in person).
  • Proof of residential address in your home country (e.g., a utility bill or bank statement not older than 3 to 6 months).
  • Proof of the origin of funds (crucial for real estate purchases). This can include tax returns, employment contracts, sale deeds of other properties, or inheritance documents.
  • A Bulgarian Personal Number (LNCh) or Foreigner ID number, depending on residency status, though some banks can open accounts using just a passport.

2. Understand the Source of Funds Rule

Because the account will be used for property acquisition, compliance officers will scrutinize where the money is coming from. Transparency is key. If you are following a Step-by-Step Guide to Buying Property in Bulgaria as a Foreigner, ensuring your financial paperwork matches your purchase agreement is vital to avoid compliance delays.

3. Visit the Branch in Person

Most Bulgarian banks require a face-to-face meeting at a physical branch to open a non-resident account. While some digital-first services exist, traditional banks prefer in-person identification. If traveling to Bulgaria is not immediately feasible, you can sometimes grant Power of Attorney (POA) to a local lawyer to open the account on your behalf, though this requires a heavily apostilled and translated legal document.

4. Pay Compliance and Setup Fees

Non-resident accounts often incur a compliance review fee, ranging from 50 to a few hundred Bulgarian Lev (BGN), depending on the bank and the complexity of your financial profile. Once compliance is cleared, the account is usually activated within 3 to 7 business days.

Special Considerations for Land Buyers

If your property purchase includes a plot of land rather than just an apartment, remember that non-EU/EEA citizens face constitutional restrictions on owning land directly in Bulgaria. Many foreign buyers bypass this limitation by establishing a local legal entity. If this applies to your situation, make sure you understand the nuances highlighted in Can Foreigners Buy Land in Bulgaria? Why You Need to Register a Company (OOD), as you will need to open a corporate bank account rather than a personal non-resident account.

Tips for a Smooth Banking Experience

  • Be Patient: Compliance departments in Bulgarian banks act conservatively. Answer all follow-up questions from your personal banker promptly and accurately.
  • Keep Translations Ready: Documents not in Bulgarian or English (such as tax declarations from non-EU nations) must be officially translated into Bulgarian by a sworn translator.
  • Clarify Online Banking Access: Ensure your mobile banking app and hardware token (if required) are fully activated before you leave the bank branch so you can manage funds remotely.

Opening a Bulgarian bank account as a non-resident requires clear preparation and absolute transparency regarding your funds. By choosing the right financial institution and preparing your paperwork in advance, you can streamline your property acquisition and enjoy a secure, legally compliant real estate investment journey in Bulgaria.
